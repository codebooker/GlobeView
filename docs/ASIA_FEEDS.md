# Asia feed rollout

Only current, located records with reusable data and a working delivery endpoint become GlobeView markers. A public map page or camera inventory by itself does not establish a live feed.

## Japan

The transport ministry publishes [five-minute traffic counts with coordinates](https://www.mlit.go.jp/report/press/road01_hh_001930.html) through the [JARTIC traffic API](https://www.jartic-open-traffic.org/). On 2 October 2026, one national five-minute request returned 958 recent measurements. However, the [API terms](https://www.jartic-open-traffic.org/xROAD%E4%BA%A4%E9%80%9A%E9%87%8FAPI%E6%A9%9F%E8%83%BD%E5%88%A9%E7%94%A8%E8%A6%8F%E7%B4%84v3.pdf) defer content-use conditions to JARTIC's [website terms](https://www.jartic.or.jp/attention/terms/), which restrict third-party public display without permission. GlobeView does not republish those counts pending a clear reuse grant. The ministry's [road restriction viewer](https://www.road-info-prvs.mlit.go.jp/roadinfo/sp/spTop_00_0.html) shows current closures, but no separate licensed feature feed was verified. Shizuoka City's former [open road-restriction API](https://www.city.shizuoka.lg.jp/s9428/s001278.html) ceased service on 30 September 2026; it cannot be used as a current source. TEPCO's [public outage map](https://teideninfo.tepco.co.jp/) reports service-area faults, but a licensed geolocated bulk feed was not verified. Current camera images, readable sign states, live emergency dispatch, and an official plate-reader inventory still need source checks.

## South Korea

The national [road-camera video catalog](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15040466/openapi.do) and expressway [real-time traffic API](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15076684/openapi.do) advertise reuse, but require a portal service key; the expressway API also requires production approval. The national [CCTV inventory](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15155042/openapi.do) gives device locations, not viewable camera feeds or confirmed plate readers. Busan publishes a [119 fire-dispatch API](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15087824/openapi.do) with event timestamps and area-level addresses, but its required service key is not configured, and those addresses need careful geocoding before map markers can be trusted. None of these are presented as live GlobeView coverage yet. Power-fault, readable-sign, and official plate-reader feeds remain to be checked.

## Hong Kong

The [Transport Department's traffic snapshot catalog](https://data.gov.hk/en-data/dataset/hk-td-tis_2-traffic-snapshot-images) provides camera coordinates and JPEGs updated about every two minutes. GlobeView maps official stills at zoom 10+, checks the images for a recent timestamp and valid JPEG before serving them, and limits health checks to at most 80 cameras per requested view. Zooming closer reveals more. One shared six-hour camera catalog and five-minute per-camera health cache avoid repeated upstream requests. On 2 October 2026, the catalog yielded 1,011 placed cameras and four sampled stills were current; these are snapshots, not video. [DATA.GOV.HK terms](https://data.gov.hk/en/terms-and-conditions) allow reuse with attribution to the Government, Transport Department, and portal.

The [current roadworks GeoJSON](https://data.gov.hk/en-data/dataset/hk-td-tis_18-roadworks-location) supplies locations, lane effects, work type, and Hong Kong-local start/end times. GlobeView maps only works marked in progress within their schedule and with a recent record update in Construction at zoom 10+. The 2 October 2026 check yielded five current works. This feed mainly covers Highways Department works closing a lane on roads with a speed limit of at least 70 km/h, plus specified Transport Department equipment works; it is not every street excavation. The [special traffic news](https://data.gov.hk/en-data/dataset/hk-td-tis_19-special-traffic-news-v2) is a text feed without verified event coordinates in this pass. No reusable current emergency-call, power-fault, readable-sign, or official plate-reader feed has yet been verified.

The [strategic and major road detector feed](https://data.gov.hk/en-data/dataset/hk-td-sm_4-traffic-data-strategic-major-roads) publishes valid-lane speed, volume, and occupancy readings. GlobeView joins its detector IDs to the official location CSV and shows readings in Road sensors at zoom 10+. The endpoint is advertised as updating every minute, but the retrieved publication lagged several minutes during verification, so GlobeView accepts readings up to 20 minutes old and rejects offline lanes. The panel shows the measurement time. On 2 October 2026, the published catalog held 807 locations and the live feed produced 763 located detectors with valid lanes. The values describe the last 30 seconds at each detector, not the whole road segment.

## Singapore

The Land Transport Authority's [traffic images](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_6cdb6b405b25aaaacbaf7689bcc6fae0/view) are available through the public data.gov.sg API. GlobeView maps only recently published cameras whose official JPEGs respond, starting at zoom 10. Clicking a camera opens a still image inside the map. One shared 90-second catalog and image cache limit upstream requests. On 2 October 2026, seven of eight published cameras had viewable recent images. These are stills, not video, and camera availability can change. Data is attributed to the Land Transport Authority via data.gov.sg under the [Singapore Open Data Licence](https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence). Current emergency-call, power-outage, readable-sign, roadworks, and official plate-reader feeds still need source checks.

## Taiwan

Taipei's Public Works Department publishes [today's construction information](https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/145614) as a frequently updated GeoJSON file. The coordinates use TWD97 / TM2 and are transformed to WGS84. GlobeView shows only current permits flagged as affecting traffic in Construction at zoom 10+, without the personal contact fields in the source. The 2 October 2026 publication contained 1,190 records; 142 distinct, located works passed the current-date and traffic-impact checks. Long-running permits may remain on the map even when no crew is present at the moment. The source is licensed under Taiwan's [Open Government Data License 1.0](https://data.gov.tw/en/license), with attribution to the Public Works Department, Taipei City Government.

Taipei's traffic engineering office publishes [sign locations](https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/135704) and [current displayed messages](https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/129029). GlobeView joins them by CMS ID and shows text from operating signs in Road signs & messages at zoom 10+. The 3 October 2026 Taipei-local publications contained 178 located signs and 177 live records; the 02:32 local snapshot had 148 signs with a healthy device and active text. The [CMS status definitions](https://thbapp.thb.gov.tw/opendata/cmsdata.aspx) mark device status 0 as normal and message status 1 as actively displaying. A missing, faulty, placeholder, or more-than-10-minute-old message is hidden. Multiple texts on one sign are shown together; a message may rotate before a user reaches the physical sign. The source is licensed under Taiwan's [Open Government Data License 1.0](https://data.gov.tw/en/license), with attribution to the Department of Transportation Engineering and Management, Taipei City.

The Highway Bureau's [provincial road equipment directory](https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/29817) links to current nationwide CCTV, CMS, and vehicle-detector publications. On 3 October 2026 Taiwan time, the official catalogs contained 2,334 located cameras, 1,176 signs, and 1,488 detectors; the live sign and detector publications were current. GlobeView displays camera stills in-map at zoom 10+, joins sign and detector observations to catalog coordinates, and drops readings older than ten minutes. Camera snapshots are fetched only when opened, checked as JPEGs, and shared for one minute. Sample cameras on each of eight official image hosts returned working JPEGs; individual cameras can still go offline. The data is attributed to the Highway Bureau under the [Open Government Data License 1.0](https://data.gov.tw/en/license).

The Freeway Bureau lists [CCTV](https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/37665) and [dynamic message signs](https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/37668) as open data, but their published `tisvcloud.freeway.gov.tw` resources timed out during verification. The older [CCTV dynamic file](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/37666) is explicitly withdrawn. The [National Police traffic reports](https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/15221) also timed out. [New Taipei's rescue map](https://e.ntpc.gov.tw/news-info/rescue) exposes current incident features, but its reuse terms and incident-level freshness need verification before republishing. The [Taiwan Power Company disaster outage feed](https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/44799) updates during disasters, not as a continuous national outage census. Taichung's advertised live detector file was last updated in March 2026; Taipei's separate detector feed was last updated in 2024, so neither is mapped. No verified live emergency-dispatch, continuous outage, or official plate-reader feed is presented for Taiwan yet.

## Malaysia

The Malaysian Highway Authority's [traffic-management camera layer](https://gis02.llm.gov.my/llmsmpt/rest/services/GIS_LLM/TMC/MapServer/0) lists 488 positions, but sampled image URLs used `vigroot.llm.gov.my`, which did not resolve, and sample record dates were from 2022. Kuala Lumpur's [KLCCC camera page](https://klccc.dbkl.gov.my/cctv-images/) lists three HLS streams; each returned HTTP 403 in the 2 October 2026 check. Neither catalog currently supports working in-map cameras. The authority's [roadblock, detour, and closure layers](https://gis02.llm.gov.my/llmsmpt/rest/services/Hosted/TMP_LLM/FeatureServer) contained only a few 2020 records, most without usable details. KLCCC's current traffic notices are prose without a verified located feed. [MyJalan](https://www.malaysia.gov.my/en/topics/myjalan-kkr-application) offers live highway CCTV and road reports through its app, but a public reusable endpoint was not verified.

The [national weather API](https://developer.data.gov.my/realtime-api/weather) publishes current warnings, but sampled strong-wind and thunderstorm warnings named states and districts without coordinates. They need trusted administrative geometry before they can be mapped accurately; the API allows [four requests per minute](https://developer.data.gov.my/rate-limit). The [999 emergency service](https://www.malaysia.gov.my/en/categories/safety-and-community/public-safety/mers-999-emergency-line) and [fire statistics dashboard](https://data.gov.my/dashboard/fire-and-rescue) do not publish a verified live, located dispatch feed. No current reusable power-outage, readable-sign, or official plate-reader feed was verified.

## Thailand

The Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation's [public disaster map](https://ddc.disaster.go.th/) uses an official [geolocated disaster layer](https://wgeo.disaster.go.th/geoserver/wfs?service=WFS&request=GetCapabilities&version=1.1.0). GlobeView adds open disaster reports to Emergency reports abroad, retaining only records with an update within three days, a start within 30 days, a valid point inside Thailand, and no end date. The source's active flag alone is insufficient: it still returned reports from 2023. On 2 October 2026, the live filtered request produced ten reports, mostly floods. These are reported affected-area points, not precise incident sites or a complete 191 emergency-call feed. The server requests a bounded recent slice every five minutes and attributes the DDPM source in each popup. The public WFS advertises no access constraints, though it does not name a specific reuse licence. Its server omits an intermediate TLS certificate; GlobeView supplies the public GlobalSign intermediate and still verifies the server certificate.

The [Department of Highways camera portal](https://highwaytraffic.go.th/DOHMobile/Modules/CCTV.aspx) lists cameras, but sampled detail pages displayed a service-down image; the alternate catalog contained records many years old. No viewable current camera feed was verified. The [Bangkok traffic camera app](https://gov.bangkok.go.th/) is described as a legacy, less-stable service. The [transport open-data catalog](https://datagov.mot.go.th/en/) contained historical camera extracts and a traffic image, not a current located feature feed. [PEA Power Map](https://www.pea.co.th/pea-power-map) and [MEA Power Map](https://measervice.mea.or.th/powermap/load/index.html) show grid capacity, not live outages. Readable signs, current road construction/incidents, live outage locations, and official plate-reader locations still need a reusable source.
