# Europe feed rollout

Start with the UK and Ireland. A map marker is added only when an authority publishes a usable location and the record is current. Public map pages, historical incident files, and sign locations alone are not treated as live feeds.

| Area | Feed | Current status / next source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| England | Emergency reports | Environment Agency flood warnings are mapped. [Fire incident records](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/fire-statistics-incident-level-datasets) are historical; a public live dispatch feed has not been verified. |
| Great Britain | Power cuts | [UK Power Networks](https://ukpowernetworks.opendatasoft.com/explore/dataset/ukpn-live-faults/), [Northern Powergrid](https://northernpowergrid.opendatasoft.com/explore/dataset/live-power-cuts-data/), [SSEN PowerTrack](https://data.ssen.co.uk/@ssen-distribution/realtime_outage_dataset), and [National Grid Electricity Distribution](https://connecteddata.nationalgrid.co.uk/dataset/live-power-cuts) are mapped in their service areas. NGED records older than two hours are hidden. [SP Energy Networks](https://spenergynetworks.opendatasoft.com/explore/dataset/distribution-network-live-outages/) and [Electricity North West](https://electricitynorthwest.opendatasoft.com/explore/dataset/sp-enw-live-customers-off-supply/api/) publish outage data but currently require registered access to their actual records. |
| London | Road incidents and works | [TfL disruptions](https://api.tfl.gov.uk/Road/all/Disruption) are mapped. TfL's [Jam Cams and live message signs](https://tfl.gov.uk/info-for/open-data-users/our-open-data) require registration for the live syndication feeds. |
| England trunk roads | Closures and signs | [National Highways](https://developer.data.nationalhighways.co.uk/) offers road/lane closures and current digital sign messages after free account registration and API subscription. Its published limit is 10 calls per key per minute. |
| Wales | Road incidents and works | [Traffic Wales RSS](https://traffic.wales/developers) is mapped for motorway/trunk-road events, with current work windows enforced. Camera images and DATEX II sign messages require [approval](https://traffic.wales/developers). |
| Scotland | Roads, signs, cameras | Current geolocated works from the [Scottish Road Works Register](https://roadworks.scot/opendata) daily disruption extract are mapped under OGL v3. [Traffic Scotland](https://www.traffic.gov.scot/traffic-scotland-developer-hub) has fresher DATEX II roadworks/events/sign messages and camera images for approved registered subscribers. |
| Northern Ireland | Cameras and roads | [TrafficWatch NI](https://www.trafficwatchni.com/twni/cameras) publishes camera images on its public site. Its [RSS feeds](https://www.trafficwatchni.com/twni/rss-faqs) publish incidents and works every five minutes under [reuse terms](https://www.trafficwatchni.com/twni/terms-and-conditions), but the records checked have no coordinates or item URLs, so they cannot yet be placed reliably on the map. A reusable camera catalog/feed still needs verification. |
| UK and Ireland | Plate-reader locations | The existing [DeFlock/OpenStreetMap](https://deflock.me/) layer covers community-mapped devices where reported. It is not a live plate-read feed or a complete inventory. No official comprehensive public ANPR location feed has been verified. |
| Republic of Ireland | Emergency calls | [Dublin Fire Brigade activity logs](https://data.gov.ie/dataset?tags=emergency-services) are annual historical data; no public live dispatch feed has been verified. |
| Republic of Ireland | Power cuts | [ESB PowerCheck](https://www.esbnetworks.ie/services/power-outages/check-my-power-status/powercheck-guidelines) publishes a live customer map. A documented reusable outage API and terms have not been verified. |
| Republic of Ireland | Cameras, signs, works | [TII's traffic map](https://traffic.tii.ie/list/cameras) shows cameras. Its [open DATEX II directory](https://data.tii.ie/Datasets/Its/DatexII/) is CC BY 4.0, but the VMS settings publication is dated 29 January 2021 and its planned/unplanned event publications are dated 28 January 2021 or earlier, so these are not live feeds. [MapRoad](https://datacatalogue.gov.ie/dataset/maproad-roadworks-licensing-system) has a national roadworks API that is explicitly not open data. |
| France | National-road incidents and works | [Bison Futé / DIR DATEX II](https://transport.data.gouv.fr/datasets/evenements-routiers-sur-le-reseau-routier-national-non-concede) is mapped for current, geolocated records under Licence Ouverte 2.0. Publication freshness and event start/end times are checked. Coverage is limited to the non-concession national network; it is not a feed for all French roads. Digital sign messages and traffic cameras are still to be evaluated separately. |
| France | Traffic sensors | [Bison Futé / DIR speed and flow measurements](https://transport.data.gouv.fr/datasets/etat-de-circulation-en-temps-reel-sur-le-reseau-national-routier-non-concede) are mapped where the station reference supplies Lambert-93 coordinates. Measurements older than 30 minutes and publications older than 45 minutes are hidden. This covers part of the non-concession national network, not every road. |
| France | Emergency calls | The national [fire and rescue intervention dataset](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/interventions-realisees-par-les-services-d-incendie-et-de-secours) is statistical history, not live geolocated dispatch. No reusable live dispatch feed has been verified. |
| France | Power cuts | [Enedis Panne et interruption](https://www.enedis.fr/panne-et-interruption) reports incidents for searched addresses or communes. A public, documented bulk outage feed with reusable coordinates has not been verified. Do not scrape the address-lookup service as a national outage API. |
| France | Cameras and digital signs | The [national transport access point](https://transport.data.gouv.fr/datasets?filter=has_realtime) publishes the Bison Futé road events and traffic measurements above. A current, reusable French road-camera image feed or live variable-message-sign display feed has not yet been verified. |
| France | Plate readers | The existing [DeFlock / OpenStreetMap](https://deflock.me/) layer can show community-mapped locations in France where reported. It does not expose live plate reads or provide a complete inventory. |

Next integration candidates are the approved National Highways, TfL, Traffic Scotland, and Traffic Wales feeds if credentials or subscriber approval are obtained. For Ireland, a current official live feed with reusable coordinates has not yet been verified; ESB's outage map and TII's camera page are public services, but their data-reuse terms or machine-readable feeds remain unclear.
