# Europe feed rollout

Start with the UK and Ireland. A map marker is added only when an authority publishes a usable location and the record is current. Public map pages, historical incident files, and sign locations alone are not treated as live feeds.

| Area | Feed | Current status / next source |

| Finland | Road weather cameras | [Fintraffic Digitraffic](https://www.digitraffic.fi/en/road-traffic/) publishes geolocated stations and timestamped stills under [CC BY 4.0](https://www.digitraffic.fi/en/terms-of-service/). GlobeView shows one recently collected still per active station at zoom 10+, omits images older than 30 minutes, and refreshes an open popup every ten minutes. Images are snapshots, not live video. |
| Finland | Traffic and road weather sensors | [Fintraffic's TMS counters](https://www.digitraffic.fi/en/road-traffic/lam/) publish directional five-minute rolling speed and vehicle-flow rates. GlobeView maps recently measured values at zoom 10+ under CC BY 4.0, omitting readings older than 15 minutes and source publications older than 20 minutes. These are station readings, not continuous traffic or weather surfaces. |

[National Highways' developer portal](https://developer.data.nationalhighways.co.uk/) offers current road/lane closures and digital sign messages after account registration and API subscription, limited to 10 calls per key per minute. |

| Republic of Ireland | Weather alerts | Current [Met Éireann warnings](https://data.gov.ie/dataset/weather-warnings) are mapped in International weather alerts. | Republic of Ireland | Road cameras, signs, works, incidents | [TII's traffic map](https://traffic.tii.ie/) currently publishes 237 active public camera records, geolocated road events, and sign displays through its anonymous public traffic API. GlobeView maps camera stills at zoom 10+, audits image freshness hourly to omit stale or missing cameras, and fetches selected images through a shared three-minute server cache. Current road events are separated into construction and incidents by the event's start/end window; future works are excluded. Signs are shown only when the actual display PNG was modified within 30 minutes, avoiding years-old images still listed as current. The API snapshot is shared across users for three minutes. Attribution follows [TII's public-sector-information licence](https://www.tii.ie/en/compliance/reuse-of-public-sector-information/) (CC BY 4.0); the required notice appears in GlobeView's source details. The older [DATEX II directory](https://data.tii.ie/Datasets/Its/DatexII/) remains stale. [MapRoad](https://datacatalogue.gov.ie/dataset/maproad-roadworks-licensing-system) is not open data. |
