# Europe feed rollout

Start with the UK and Ireland. A map marker is added only when an authority publishes a usable location and the record is current. Public map pages, historical incident files, and sign locations alone are not treated as live feeds.

[Vegagerðin's official road-camera API](https://www.vegagerdin.is/vegagerdin/gagnasafn/vefthjonustur/vefmyndavelar) publishes geolocated still-image URLs. GlobeView maps one pin per camera station at zoom 10+, checks a current image for each station every 15 minutes, and lets viewers switch between its two to four camera directions in the popup. On 27 September 2026 the API listed 497 images at 165 stations; all checked image headers were under 30 minutes old. The stills update several times per hour, not continuously. The authority's [web-service terms](https://www.vegagerdin.is/vegagerdin/gagnasafn/vefthjonustur/skilmalar-vefthjonustur) license the images and data under CC BY 4.0. The [official DATEX II situation service](https://www.vegagerdin.is/vegagerdin/gagnasafn/vefthjonustur/datexii-2) adds current point incidents and roadworks; GlobeView checks publication and event validity, then separates works from other road hazards. The 27 September 2026 snapshot contained 13 works and 31 other active incidents. The same DATEX service publishes geolocated roadside weather stations and traffic counters. GlobeView joins current measurements to official station locations in Road sensors, showing air and road temperature and ten-minute vehicle counts where available. It excludes readings older than 30 minutes and fault-marked sensors; the checked snapshot had 184 current stations. Road-condition reports are joined to the authority's predefined road sections and placed at approximate section centers in Road incidents. GlobeView uses the readable passability text for generic DATEX `roadClosed` records because that code is also assigned to sections described as easily passable; it separately labels winter closures, fog, slush, and loose chippings. Validity windows and publication age are checked. The checked snapshot had 115 active section reports. These pins represent sections, not precise incident locations.
