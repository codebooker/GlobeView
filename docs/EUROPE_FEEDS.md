# Europe feed rollout

Start with the UK and Ireland. A map marker is added only when an authority publishes a usable location and the record is current. Public map pages, historical incident files, and sign locations alone are not treated as live feeds.

A reusable camera catalog/feed still needs verification. |

Publication freshness and event start/end times are checked. Coverage is limited to the non-concession national network; it is not a feed for all French roads. Digital sign messages and traffic cameras are still to be evaluated separately. |

| France | Cameras and digital signs | The [national transport access point](https://transport.data.gouv.fr/datasets?filter=has_realtime) publishes the Bison Futé road events and traffic measurements above. Next integration candidates are the approved National Highways, TfL, Traffic Scotland, and Traffic Wales feeds if credentials or subscriber approval are obtained. For Ireland, a current official live feed with reusable coordinates has not yet been verified; ESB's outage map and TII's camera page are public services, but their data-reuse terms or machine-readable feeds remain unclear.

## Belgium

The [Flemish Traffic Centre DATEX II v3 full feed](https://www.verkeerscentrum.be/data) is mapped for current roadworks and other road restrictions under its Modellicentie Gratis Hergebruik. The server converts its Lambert 72 geometry to WGS84, uses one representative point per situation, and checks publication freshness and event validity. Where its [OTAP feed](https://www.verkeerscentrum.be/uitwisseling/otap) has a matching ID, the marker also names the road. The source says the XML refreshes each minute; GlobeView shares one cached snapshot for three minutes. This covers Flemish roads published by the authority, not every Belgian road. The current feed did not contain a separate live sign-message or camera-image catalog.

Local roadwork effects are also mapped from [GIPOD planned mobility hindrance](https://geo.api.vlaanderen.be/GIPOD/ogc/features/v1/openapi?f=text%2Fhtml), published by Digitaal Vlaanderen under the [free-reuse model licence](https://www.vlaanderen.be/datavindplaats/catalogus/gipod-geplande-mobiliteitshinder-publieke-informatie-ldes). GlobeView asks only for small map tiles and current validity windows, then keeps validated work zones with motor-traffic consequences. A shared five-minute tile cache limits upstream traffic. These are planned or reported work impacts, not live traffic sensors or a guarantee that the road is currently impassable.

[Brussels Mobility's event collection](https://data.mobility.brussels/en/info/27a5c729-80c9-42fc-9454-a278a56a5ca2/) offers current geolocated events under CC0, but its metadata says service use needs a bearer token; do not depend on anonymous browser access for production. [Fluvius](https://www.fluvius.be/nl/storingen-en-werken/stroomonderbrekingen) and [Sibelga](https://www.sibelga.be/en/outages-streetworks/power-outages/actual-power-outages) show current outages publicly; a documented reusable bulk outage feed remains to be checked. No public live emergency dispatch or official plate-reader inventory has been verified; the existing community-mapped DeFlock layer remains available.

## Netherlands

[NDW Open Data](https://opendata.ndw.nu/) provides minute-scale DATEX II publications for current road situations and dynamic road signs. GlobeView maps active incidents and roadworks from `actueel_beeld.xml.gz` and working signs with a current text or PNG display from `dynamische_route_informatie_paneel.xml.gz`. Each road situation becomes one representative point; WGS84 line positions arrive as latitude then longitude. Publication age and validity windows are checked, and all viewers share a three-minute server cache. The feed reflects roads reported to NDW, not every street. Emergency calls, utility outages, and a reusable live traffic-camera image feed still need verification. Community-mapped DeFlock locations remain available for plate-reader locations; they are not live reads.

## Germany

The [Autobahn GmbH road API](https://verkehr.autobahn.de/o/autobahn/) publishes geolocated roadworks, warnings, and closures by motorway. GlobeView maps records marked current, excludes future starts, and shares one five-minute warning cache and fifteen-minute roadwork/closure caches across visitors. On the feed checked 27 September 2026, 109 motorways yielded 19 current warnings, 372 closures, and 1,834 roadworks; these counts vary. Coverage is the Autobahn network, not all German roads. Sample motorway webcam lists were empty, and this API does not expose readable dynamic sign displays. A reusable national live emergency-call or power-outage feed has not been verified. The [Federal Network Agency interruption data](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Fachthemen/ElektrizitaetundGas/Versorgungssicherheit/Versorgungsunterbrechungen/start.html) are annual operator reports, not a current outage map. The BBK [states that NINA's app files are not a public warning API](https://fragdenstaat.de/anfrage/verfuegbarkeit-warnung-bund-de-und-api/); do not ingest those as a production dispatch feed. Community-mapped DeFlock locations remain available for plate-reader locations, without live read data.

## Austria

[LSZ Burgenland](https://einsatz.lsz-b.at/) publishes current fire brigade dispatches and [permits embedding its public view with source credit](https://www.lsz-b.at/bos-digitalfunk/). GlobeView maps ongoing calls whose place exactly matches one of the 171 official Burgenland municipality names. Coordinates are [Statistics Austria's 2025 municipality population centers](https://data.statistik.gv.at/web/meta.jsp?dataset=OGDEXT_GEM_MP_1), under CC BY 4.0; markers are approximate town locations, not incident addresses. The server rejects source pages older than 20 minutes and shares a five-minute snapshot. Smaller places without a matching municipality are omitted rather than guessed.

[ASFINAG's traffic portal](https://contentportal.asfinag.at/) offers free registered DATEX II feeds for current motorway/expressway incidents, construction, and variable sign states; access requires account registration and accepting its [licence and interface terms](https://contentportal.asfinag.at/assets/licenses/cc-by-40-asf/en/cc-by-40-asf.html). [EVIS.AT](https://evis.asfinag.at/) aggregates events on other key Austrian roads but also requires registration; the [national access-point listing](https://mobilitydata.gv.at/daten/ungeplante-ereignismeldungen-evisat) describes contractual access and a usage fee. These feeds are not yet mapped. Tyrol's public [traffic camera viewer](https://verkehrsinformation.tirol.gv.at/web/html/wc.html) exposes a machine-readable catalog: the 27 September 2026 check returned 112 state-road cameras with distinct coordinates and images, plus 67 ASFINAG cameras whose coordinates cluster near one point and cannot be mapped as supplied. The images refreshed successfully, but [Tyrol's website terms](https://www.tirol.gv.at/impressum/) say reuse beyond personal information needs a request; the open-data licence applies only to datasets on its [OGD portal](https://www.tirol.gv.at/data/nutzungsbedingungen/). The camera catalog is therefore a ready integration candidate once reuse is approved, not yet a GlobeView feed. [ASFINAG's public webcams](https://staging.asfinag.at/verkehr-sicherheit/webcams/) also need a reusable coordinate/image catalog and applicable terms. No documented reusable live power-outage API or official plate-reader catalog has been verified; the community-mapped DeFlock layer remains available where reported.

## Switzerland

[Zurich canton's roadworks dataset](https://data.stadt-zuerich.ch/dataset/d991a4a2-32ea-4f7a-93b5-0f31a016d71c) publishes point locations, start and end dates, and traffic guidance under CC0. GlobeView maps works marked active and within their scheduled dates in the Construction layer; this covers cantonal roads in Zurich, not all Swiss roadworks. The shared server snapshot refreshes every three minutes. Zurich's [traffic-counter locations](https://data.stadt-zuerich.ch/dataset/ktzh_verkehrsmessstellen__ogd_) are CC0 and join by station number to its [open live counter API](https://datenkatalog.statistik.zh.ch/datasets/692@tiefbauamt-kanton-zuerich), licensed CC BY 4.0. GlobeView shows active counters in Road sensors with the labelled year of their published daily average; selecting a marker requests one recent vehicle detection. A detection is not a speed or traffic-flow estimate. The server limits and briefly caches these on-demand requests rather than opening streams for every mapped counter. The federal [Traffic Data Platform](https://www.astra.admin.ch/en/mobility-data) publishes [current road incidents and roadworks as DATEX II](https://opentransportdata.swiss/en/cookbook/road-traffic-cookbook/traffic-situations/), but the SOAP pull interface requires a registered API key. It recommends one full snapshot per day followed by incremental requests no more than five minutes apart. Records are referenced to the Swiss TMC/Alert-C topology; the mapping table must be requested separately, so an API key alone is insufficient to place them accurately. Public utility outage pages such as [BKW's](https://outage.bkw.ch/) are address lookups rather than verified reusable bulk feeds. No public live dispatch, traffic-camera image catalog, readable sign-state feed, or official plate-reader inventory has yet been verified.

## Iceland

The [Icelandic Meteorological Office CAP API](https://api.vedur.is/) publishes active public hazard warnings with polygons under CC BY 4.0. GlobeView maps an approximate point for each current alert polygon, excluding expired and cleared messages, and credits the office and retrieval date as its [terms require](https://en.vedur.is/about-imo/the-web/conditions). These are weather and natural-hazard alerts, not live emergency dispatch calls. Other requested road, camera, outage, and plate-reader feeds remain to be assessed.
