    Roads
    Public safety & utilities
    Coverage & sources

    Coverage varies by feed. Aircraft, ports, hazards, and scan sources are global; emergency reports abroad cover parts of Australia, New Zealand, and England. Vessels need an AISStream key and zoom 5+.

    FIRMS marks heat detections; GDELT locations are approximate; hazard points are events, not footprints. Satellite imagery is not live. Sea conditions are forecasts. Scan locations are not confirmed attacks. Aircraft are thinned at globe scale.

    Alerts use source polygons when available; some U.S. markers represent forecast zones or counties. Open an alert for official details. Outage feeds cover participating utilities, including parts of Great Britain: UK Power Networks, Northern Powergrid, SSEN, and National Grid. Supported by NGED Open Data.

    Road feeds cover North America and parts of Europe. Sources include Fintraffic, TfL, Traffic Wales, Scottish Road Works Register, Bison Futé / DIR, the Flemish Traffic Centre, GIPOD work zones, and NDW Netherlands. DeFlock reader locations are community mapped, not live plate reads.
